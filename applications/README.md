# Application-focused granular-ball research

This branch searches for reproducible application settings where a simple
granular-ball augmentation improves strong conventional machine-learning
models. Theory expansion and ornamental module stacking are out of scope.

Priority domains:

1. industrial fault diagnosis;
2. credit risk and financial fraud;
3. IIoT/network intrusion detection;
4. medical diagnosis only as a backup.

Candidate GB roles are structural features, cross-fitted reliability weights,
noise suppression, imbalance handling, few-shot aggregation, boundary
augmentation, and (only after a stable gain) three-way training routing.
