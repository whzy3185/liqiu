## Cross-cutting synthesis

Three independent evidence streams converge.

1. **Changing the name of the representation does not create a new mechanism.**
   Radius-offset routing is established weighted-site geometry; fixed-tree
   purity selection is a restricted pruning path; and free-prototype complexity
   results cannot be adopted without respecting the hierarchy construction.
2. **A valid attribution control is decisive.** Matched CART, centre-only,
   construction routing, isotropic-region, and standard pruning controls either
   explain the observed effect or expose the missing assumption. A raw GB
   improvement, visual boundary change, or nonadditive example is insufficient.
3. **Reproducibility is a first-class gate.** The EMBER cache cannot be assigned
   a binary target without a row map; the AD-GBC source cannot be called an
   unchanged MPS reproduction; and a theoretical decision problem cannot be
   called NP-complete without an honest verifier and construction-respecting
   reduction.

The apparent tension between the older empirical failure signals and the new
audit closures is resolved: the failures are real diagnostics of particular GB
implementations, while the proposed repairs/contributions either lack an
exclusive mechanism or cannot meet their reproducibility contract. A negative
diagnostic is not automatically a publishable new method.

## What would require new authority

No automatic continuation is authorized from this point. Exactly one of the
following materially changes the research contract:

1. **Ported-reproduction authority.** Permit a documented, behavior-preserving
   MPS port of the MIT AD-GBC training infrastructure and allow acquisition of a
   named licensed target dataset. This would be a new *ported reproduction*
   candidate, not an unchanged-author-code reproduction; it must restart at
   Phase 1.
2. **CUDA reproduction authority.** Provide an accessible CUDA environment and
   allow the official AD-GBC/KSDD2 reproduction protocol. This changes only the
   execution environment but still yields a bounded transfer artifact, not a
   new method.
3. **Topic-change authority.** Move beyond granular-ball research. The present
   evidence does not justify inventing another GB application to keep the
   campaign running.

## Synthesis limitations

- The closure is a branch-level decision, not a proof that no future
  granular-ball contribution is possible.
- Several negative results are clean-room/repository-specific and cannot rank
  unavailable author implementations.
- The audits are targeted mechanism reviews, not exhaustive systematic reviews.
- The untracked local `data/` and prior result paths were preserved and excluded
  from branch artifacts.
