# Purity Contamination Theory Decision

Decision: `KILL_PURITY_CONTAMINATION_THEORY`.

The fixed-tree model yields correct diagnostic formulas: exact tau-one frontier
characterization, single-flip depth amplification, and a tight complete-tree
marked-prefix count. Exhaustive small-instance checks did not find a
counterexample within the stated assumptions.

But the S1 gate requires more than correctness. The strongest result is the
standard count of mixed ancestors or the union of marked root-to-leaf paths in
a binary hierarchy, translated into purity-pruning terminology. The
tau-less-than-one condition is elementary threshold algebra, and no new
multi-scale fidelity--robustness--compression lower bound was obtained.

Therefore neither recursive amplification nor exact characterization clears the
novelty requirement in a granular-ball theory paper. Do not start controlled
verification, UCI benchmarks, topology experiments, a new robust GBC method,
or further threshold search. Preserve the formalization and counterexamples as
negative research artifacts.
