# Experiment

- Data: Adult, Bank Marketing, German Credit, Breast Cancer, Covertype.
- Exploration cap: 2,000 records per dataset.
- Seeds: 1, 7, 21, 42, 2026.
- Releases: R0-R8 from the research brief.
- Matched controls: KMeans, Ward hierarchical clustering, balanced random
  groups, and decision-tree leaves.
- Attacks: membership inference, sensitive-attribute inference, and prototype
  reconstruction.
- Utility: nearest released-prototype classification accuracy and macro-F1.
- Formal status: empirical only; no DP or cryptographic claim.
